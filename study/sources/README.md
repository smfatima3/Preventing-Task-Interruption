# HRS research planning package

This package supports a four-page double-blind workshop extended abstract.
It is a research plan, not a completed experiment report or a submission-ready manuscript.

- HRS_Research_Plan.pdf: readable full report.
- HRS_Research_Plan.md: editable full report.
- Literature_Review_25_Papers.md: 25 annotated papers with primary links.
- Experiments_and_Metrics.md: method scope, comparators, metrics, statistics, experiment sequence, and paper outline.
- references.bib: 25 portable BibTeX records using verified title, author information, year, and source URL. Some long author lists are abbreviated with BibTeX `and others`; records deliberately avoid unverified page or issue metadata.
- literature_records.json: the same review in machine-readable form.

No experimental numbers have been generated. All sample budgets and development targets are proposed choices.
The review cutoff is 30 September 2026; VIGOR is pinned to its March version, and October 2026 work is excluded from the 25-paper count.
Workshop requirements are taken from the user's supplied call. Deadline: 5 November 2026 at 23:59 AoE, or 6 November at 16:59 Pakistan time.
